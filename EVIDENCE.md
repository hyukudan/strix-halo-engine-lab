## Rule for new entries

Every new entry must identify the artifact, command or test, environment
conditions and affected specification requirement. If a measurement contradicts
an earlier entry, preserve both and record the resolution in `DECISIONS.md`.
Comparative records and private host inventories remain outside Git under the
[publication policy](AGENTS.md).
