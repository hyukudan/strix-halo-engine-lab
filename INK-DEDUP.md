## Implemented bindings

- CUDA grouped projection uses `run_staged` and typed fragment access in `src/cuda/core/mma_tile.cuh`. The BF16 tile binds the packed decoder and ordered K16 accumulation. HIP grouped projection provides `TileBinding<Format>` to `src/core/f16_tile_gemm.hpp`. The packed R1 decode retains its FMA order, shares the GEMV lane traversal/reduction, and uses the grouped activation and combine primitives.
- Both backends use the checked router in `src/moe_core/route_wave_kernel.hpp` and CSR materialization in `src/moe_core/device_grouped_plan.hpp`. The model binds score math, correction bias, selection order and route scale in `inkling2_mini_moe.hpp`, and dimensions, format choice and activation caps in `inkling2_mini_moe_dispatch.hpp`.
- CUDA attention shares BF16 cache store and flash. HIP uses the shared dense flash mainloop. `inkling2_mini_attention_prepare.hpp` supplies the relative projection, head dimensions, Q/K clipping and convolution options. The backend prepare adapters bind value normalization and the head softcap. `src/attn_core/prepared_gqa.hpp` owns the prepared-row sequence.
- `src/core/short_convolution.hpp` shares ordered tap accumulation with CUDA and HIP Qwen GDN. Projected coefficients, residual weight and BF16 storage are policy inputs to the shared convolution. `src/core/pointwise.hpp` serves the CUDA residual add and both backends' row transforms. `src/core/residual_mix.hpp` implements learned softmax bank mixing using the shared norm reductions and pointwise updates. Inkling supplies its bank selection, low-rank controller parameters and tensor names from `inkling2_mini_residual_device.hpp` and `inkling2_mini_controller_device.hpp`. The learned bank mixer currently has one production adopter.

All 15 audited GPU paths were removed, along with the redundant HIP short-convolution launcher. The implementation changes 65 files, with 2,037 lines removed and 1,938 inserted before this audit and the handoff notes. [Device verification targets](NOTES.inkb.md)
